# Brand

## Name

**Adept** (`adept.gg`).

Means someone skilled at their craft. Built for gamers, game coaches, and squads who want fast comms and 60fps screen sharing without a bloated client.

## Tone

Sharp, confident, and direct. Confident enough for a coach sending a session link to a student, with a dry anti-bloat edge aimed at heavier chat apps without relying on meme slang.

**Rule:** the edge only works if the app is solid.

## Taglines

- Gamers and coaches: "Play, coach, and share your screen at 60fps. Free."
- Dev / open source early adopters: "Fast, light, and yours."

## Ideas

- In-app RAM counter showing live memory footprint.
- Side-by-side video: 720p30 vs 60fps source quality.
- Direct VOD and replay file sharing with no size caps.
