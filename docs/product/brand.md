# Brand (ideas)

## Tone

Troll and irreverent, poking fun at Discord **without using their name or brand**.
**Rule:** the trolling only works if the app is solid.

## Taglines

- General public: "Play and share your screen at 60fps with your friends. Free."
- Dev / open source early adopters: "The Discord that's yours."

## Ideas

- In-app RAM counter: "Discord would be using 1.4 GB right now."
- Side-by-side video: 720p30 vs 60fps.
- Monkey mascot 🐒.
- Meme-style patch notes and error messages.
- "Import from Discord — run while you can" button.

## Names (availability not checked)

Monke, Yap, Bonk, Copium, Ratio, Skill Issue, Bruh.
Favourites: **Monke** or **Yap**. Not decided.
