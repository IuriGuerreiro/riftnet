Here's what works today.

Screen share that keeps up

Share your game at up to 1080p and 60 frames a second, so whoever's watching sees every frame, not a smeared replay.

Share Play: hand over the mouse

Ask to control a friend's shared screen. They allow it, you drive their keyboard and mouse. Either of you can stop it at any time. The host shares a whole screen from the desktop app.
